# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
people.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work. "This function is hard to follow" is useful;
  "you write unreadable code" is not.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Take a disagreement to its technical substance. If it cannot be settled on the
  merits, a maintainer decides and the discussion ends.
- Respect that people contribute on their own time and in their second or third
  language.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of
a discussion, publishing someone's private information, or unwelcome attention
of any kind. This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and any
space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository,
which reaches the maintainers privately, or by contacting a maintainer directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. A maintainer who is the subject of a report
is not involved in handling it.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate. Where a ban is
issued, the reason is recorded and given to the person concerned.
